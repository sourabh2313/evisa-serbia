Register an account with a username and password

Identification document (ID card or passport)

Attach a read, scanned or photographed ID card or passport.
If you attach your ID card, you must take a photo and attach both sides.

If you want the data to be automatically read, please attach a clear photo of your ID card or passport.

Attach documents
Maximum two documents, 3MB each.
Allowed formats: .pdf, .png, .jpg.

Username and password

Enter a password that will contain at least eight and at most 20 characters, at least one uppercase and lowercase letter, a number and a special character (!, @, #, $, %, &, *)
Find out who is eCitizen? More details
1

Register an account with a username and password

  • You need to attach a clear photo of your passport or ID card for foreign citizens.
  • After registration, we will send you an email with a link to confirm your email address within 24 hours.
  • We will review the submitted data and approve your registration request within 48 hours.
  • Logging in with a username and password allows you to access basic eGovernment services.
2

Activate the ConsentID mobile application

Signing in with the ConsentID mobile application is a sign-in method with a high level of reliability and allows access to all eGovernment services.

3

Activate a qualified electronic certificate in the cloud

Qualified electronic certificate in the cloud allows you to use your qualified electronic signature remotely via ConsentID.
